Ethnographic fieldwork documenting pilgrimage routes and temple festivals across Chiang Mai and
Lamphun provinces, part of an ongoing project on the ritual production of sacred landscape in
northern Thailand. The photographs record festival preparations, ritual specialists, and the
seasonal movement of devotees between upland and lowland temples.
