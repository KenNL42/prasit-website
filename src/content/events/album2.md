Photographs from the international conference *Memory, Heritage and the Politics of the Past in
Mainland Southeast Asia*, hosted by the Department of Anthropology, Chiang Mai University. The
event brought together anthropologists, historians and heritage practitioners to discuss
contested commemorative practices and the stewardship of religious heritage in the region.
