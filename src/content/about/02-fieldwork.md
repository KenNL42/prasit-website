My primary fieldwork has been carried out in northern Thailand and the upper Mekong region, where I
have conducted long-term ethnographic research since 2007. I am particularly interested in material
culture: the ways that objects — Buddha images, amulets, monuments, archives — carry memory across
generations and become the focus of religious and civic devotion.
