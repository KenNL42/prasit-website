Being in the academic community of Chiang Mai University brought me into contact with foreign
scholars who came to study Thai society, especially the anthropologists then interested in
hill-tribe research. One of them was Professor Charles F. Keyes of the University of Washington in
Seattle. After meeting and conversing with him several times, I asked for information and applied
to study for a doctorate under his supervision. In the autumn of 1995, I began doctoral studies in
Cultural Anthropology at the University of Washington, Seattle, supported initially by a Ford
Foundation fellowship (through Chiang Mai University) and later by the Wenner-Gren Foundation for
Anthropological Research, and I completed my PhD in the spring of 2001.
