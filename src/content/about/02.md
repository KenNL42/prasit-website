One day in early 1970, communist soldiers surrounded our small village. Fortunately, the village
leaders managed to escape, and we took temporary refuge at Ban Bo Yuak in Bo Kluea Nuea
Sub-district. After several days, my father — who was the village headman at the time and had good
relations with the district chief (*kamnan*) and the local Thai soldiers — decided to take me on a
military helicopter down to the hill-tribe evacuation centre at Ban Pa Klang, Sila Laeng
Sub-district, Pua District. That centre had been receiving Hmong and Iu Mien people fleeing the
communists from their mountain villages since early 1968. In the following month, my mother and my
older siblings walked down to join us at Pa Klang.
