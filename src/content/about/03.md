In May 1970, when the school term began, my father sent my older brother and me to study at Ban Pa
Klang School. I studied there from Grade 1 through Grade 7. Then, in 1977, I went on to
Matthayom 1 at Pua School, five kilometres from Ban Pa Klang. The journey was very difficult in
those days, so I asked to stay at the Christian hostel in Pua town in order to attend Pua School,
and I completed Matthayom 5 there in early 1982.
