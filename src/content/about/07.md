In 1988, I began my career as a research assistant to Dr. Peter Kunstadter of the University of
California, San Francisco, in a study of Hmong population, economy and society in Chiang Mai. At
the same time, I passed the civil service examination, completed the training, and was appointed
as a district officer (*palat amphoe*). However, I chose the academic path instead, because it
seemed to offer more freedom in my work. The following year I came to know Dr. Chayan
Vaddhanaphuti, Director of the Social Research Institute at Chiang Mai University, and worked as
his research assistant on hill-tribe research projects. I was later appointed as a civil servant
in the position of researcher in 1991.
