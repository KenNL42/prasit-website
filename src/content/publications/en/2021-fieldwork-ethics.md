This chapter examines the ethical dilemmas that arise when ethnographic research is conducted in communities still recovering from political violence. Drawing on collaborative fieldwork in Thailand's southern border provinces, the authors discuss informed consent under conditions of insecurity, the management of sensitive testimony, and the researcher's obligations to interlocutors after departure from the field.
